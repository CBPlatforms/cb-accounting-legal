Privacy Policy — CB Accounting

Effective date: June 25, 2026. Operated by Coinbound ("we", "us"). Contact: ty@coinbound.io.

CB Accounting is an internal bookkeeping-automation tool used by Coinbound to read and organize our own accounting records in QuickBooks Online (QBO). This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how we protect it.

What data we access

Through Intuit's QuickBooks Online API, authorized via OAuth 2.0, the tool accesses our company's accounting data: profit-and-loss reports, transactions (invoices, purchases, bills, journal entries), accounts, classes, locations, customers, and vendors. The tool is used only with accounting files that Coinbound owns and controls.

How we store and process it

  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in Google Cloud Secret Manager. They are never written to disk in plaintext or committed to source control.
  • Processing runs inside Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Functions and BigQuery) within an access-controlled project, restricted to least-privilege service accounts.
  • Application endpoints are private and require authentication; they are not exposed publicly.

How we use it

Solely for internal bookkeeping support: classifying transactions by department, producing departmental profit-and-loss reporting, and related reconciliation. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this accounting data with third parties. Data is handled by Coinbound and by Google Cloud as our infrastructure provider, and by Intuit as the source system, under their respective terms.

Retention

Derived status data is retained only as long as needed for bookkeeping operations. OAuth tokens are revoked when the integration is disconnected.

Your choices

An administrator can disconnect the integration at any time from within QuickBooks Online, which revokes the tool's access.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.
